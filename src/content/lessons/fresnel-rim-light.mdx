Fresnel rim light makes the edge of a surface glow when it turns away from the camera.

<FadeIn delay={0.1}>
  A simple way to think about it: surfaces facing you stay calm, surfaces viewed
  from the side get brighter.
</FadeIn>

The useful mental model is:

```text
View Direction + Normal → Fresnel → Emission
```

The common shader shape is:

```text
pow(1 - dot(normal, viewDir), rimPower)
```

This lesson uses a camera-facing sphere preview, so the view comparison is
represented by how close each point is to the silhouette edge.

## What Each Step Does

**View direction** is the direction from the surface toward the camera.

**Surface normal** is the direction the surface is facing.

**Fresnel** compares those two directions. The result is low when the surface
faces the camera and high near the silhouette edge.

**Rim power** controls how wide or sharp the glow band feels.

**Emission** adds the rim color back into the final effect.

## Three Useful Presets

- **Soft shield:** broad rim for protective bubbles, shields, and selection outlines.
- **Sharp aura:** tight stylized glow around a character or object.
- **Warm dissolve edge:** a rim-like glow that can connect to burn and dissolve effects.

## How This Connects

- The <LessonLink slug="uv-dissolve-shader">dissolve lesson</LessonLink> lights a thin emissive edge band too — both highlight a border, one driven by view angle and one by a noise threshold.
- The rim value is just another grayscale mask, so it can feed a <LessonLink slug="masked-color-ramp">masked color ramp</LessonLink> to build multi-color rims instead of a single tint.

## Common Mistakes

- Setting rim power too high, which makes the glow disappear into a thin line.
- Using too much emission, which can flatten the object into a neon sticker.
- Treating this as a real engine shader; this is a conceptual web lesson, not a Unity or ASE asset.

## Practice

Start with **Soft shield**, then raise rim power until the rim becomes thin. Switch to a warm color and describe whether it feels more like protection, heat, or dissolve energy.
