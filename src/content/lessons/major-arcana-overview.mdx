The Major Arcana tells one story: the journey of a soul from innocent beginning to full integration. Each card is a stage, not an isolated symbol.

Reading them in order — 0 through 21 — reveals the pattern. The Fool sets out. The early cards (I–VII) build identity and will. The middle cards (VIII–XIV) test and refine it. The later cards (XV–XXI) break it down and rebuild it into something whole.

## The Journey in Three Acts

**Act I — Building the Self (0–VII)**
The Fool enters the world and encounters the tools of consciousness: willpower, intuition, nurture, structure, tradition, choice, and drive. These cards answer: *who am I, and what can I do?*

**Act II — Testing the Self (VIII–XIV)**
The journey turns inward. Strength, reflection, fate, justice, surrender, transformation, and moderation. These cards answer: *what do I truly believe, and what must I release?*

**Act III — Dissolving and Becoming (XV–XXI)**
The shadow, the sudden collapse, and then — hope, mystery, joy, awakening, and finally wholeness. These cards answer: *what was I becoming all along?*

## How to Use This Map

A single card is a prompt, not a verdict. When a card appears in a reading, the useful question is not "what does this predict?" but "where in my life does this theme live right now?"

The keywords on each card are starting points. The art holds more than any word list can carry.
